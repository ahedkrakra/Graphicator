FAQ

How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall the app. Progress is stored locally on your device.

Does the Daily Challenge sync between devices?
Not currently — your daily score is stored locally per device.

Why are some levels locked?
Levels unlock as you complete the previous one. Beat level 5 to unlock level 6, and so on.

How do I get 3 stars?
Solve the level in par moves exactly. Par is shown at the top of every level.

Privacy

Popchain does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Game progress is stored only on your device.
